# Test #2 for icon-drawing: a full 54-icon set for a parenting app, in Figma

- **Dates:** 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16
- **Skill tested:** `icon-drawing` at a160fa5 (after test #1's refinements)
- **Run by:** the user, in Figma, through Claude with the skill loaded. A second "coordinator" agent did QA passes.
- **Subject:** a complete product icon set: 54 icons in 11 packs of about 5, covering the tab bar, logging, diaper types, entry actions, time and growth, sync, navigation, sharing, an AI-assistant area, safety and settings. Each icon was drawn at 24 and 20, each with a line and a filled version, then packaged as Figma components (`Style = Line | Filled`). The user approved each pack before the next started, except packs 7–11, which were drawn while they were away and reviewed afterwards.
- **Final style card:** canvas 24, stroke 1.8 (20: 1.5, 16: 1.2), round caps and joins, padding 2, keylines circle 20 / square 18 / portrait 16×20 / landscape 20×16, corners outer 3 / inner 1, gap 2, filled = the line icon's outer shape with cut-outs at stroke − 0.25. Line by default, filled for the selected tab only.

## Verdict

The skill carried a full production set. Geometry stayed clean throughout: planning in coordinates, the gap rule and the pair check kept every icon consistent. The blind test caught every metaphor failure, and none of these would have been caught by geometry checks:
- a ruler that read as a battery;
- a head that read as fruit;
- a card that read as a wallet;
- a pause that read as "00";
- an eye-off that read as "Ø";
- a photo line that read as a slash;
- a crescent with a star that formed a religious symbol;
- a flag that read as "danger".

The skill's weaknesses were rules it didn't have. There was nothing on slashes, combining two objects, body-part metaphors, crossing strokes, cultural combinations, or stroke calibration against text. Each gap cost a redraw. The checker also had one real bug.

## Findings by area

### Checker
1. **`--pair` compared a file with itself.** The set's files share basenames across folders (`line/24/x.svg`, `filled/24/x.svg`). The checker looked results up by basename, so both sides of the pair resolved to the line file. That made "filled barely heavier" fire on every pair and the silhouette match pass trivially. **The pair check never actually ran in this test**; the outlines matched because the Figma recipe produced them correctly.
2. **Crossing strokes errored.** A plain × and a globe's equator crossing its meridian were reported as "inks overlap without meeting". The workaround was to split paths at the crossing.
3. **Already-solid icons triggered the solid-share warning.** Pause (two solid bars) is 100% solid by design and has no filled form.
4. **Gap warnings gave no location.** Shrinking a detail at 20px became trial and error (four drop sizes tried).
5. **Exact-copy filled siblings** (for icons with no filled form) triggered both the mixed-modes and the "barely heavier" warnings.

### Metaphor
6. **Body-part metaphors failed twice.** A breastfeeding figure read as a smiley face and as body-like. A head with a tape read as fruit and as the letters "a" or "q". The tool or object worked both times: a milk drop in a heart, and a tape measure.
7. **There was no privacy check.** For a parenting app, the user rejected anything body-like that a mother might not want seen on her screen. Step 1 had no prompt for that.
8. **The hand-gesture ban was too broad.** Thumbs up and down are the standard rating control in AI chat and positive in the target market.
9. **Innocent shapes combined into a religious symbol.** A crescent with a four-point star was proposed for night mode. The blind test flagged it.
10. **Context decides the convention.** "Send" in an AI chat is commonly an arrow in a circle; in mail it's a paper plane. The user chose the plane, and it had to lose its fold line (read as a minus or a location arrow).
11. **Shared motifs within the set.** Undo and "earlier time" both curve back into an arrow. They needed different silhouettes (open arrow against closed circle).
12. **Combining two objects in one icon** ("wet and dirty") took three passes. Two half-size objects were too small; a 90% main object with a small solid badge in the free corner worked.
13. **Organic objects drawn with the style's stepped corners looked blocky.** Stool tiers with flat ledges read as soft-serve, cloud or bowl. Smooth bulging outlines that pinch between tiers fixed it.

### Construction
14. **The stroke read bold beside text.** At 20, 1.75 was heavier than the regular-weight labels. The user picked 1.5 and scaled the others by the same ratio (0.075 of the canvas → 1.8 at 24, 1.2 at 16). All 35 existing line icons were regenerated, and none needed a redraw, since a thinner stroke only widens gaps.
15. **Cuts through the edge chipped the silhouette.** Test #1 found that cuts running past the edge read well. In this test, a QA pass found they break the rim, and four filled icons were fixed by restoring the outer rim after cutting. The rule is now reversed: cuts stay inside.
16. **The slash had no cut width.** 3× stroke ate the eye. About 2.3× stroke (4.2 at 1.8) worked, with the slash running 0.75 past the base. A filled slashed icon stopped reading, so filled = copy.
17. **The stroke-trick dot rendered as a ring in Figma.** A heart's dot then read as a nipple. Filled circles work everywhere.
18. **Punctuation dots at 1.5× stroke looked "weirdly big".** About 1.15× worked for "?" and "!". The "?" also needed a closed round bowl, or it read as "2".
19. **Scaling 24 → 20 by 5/6 worked as a starting point** once the stroke ratio was constant. Only photo (two small hills tangling into an "M") needed its own drawing. Decorative curls that touch a curve merged at 20 and had to be dropped.
20. **Some fills destroy the object.** A filled lens reads as a ball on a stick, a filled globe as a ball, a filled clock face hides its hands. These became copies.

## Refinements made to the skill (generic rules only)

| # | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 5 | Pair bug, copy siblings | Checker: pairs are matched by path, and duplicate basenames are shown with their folder. Identical files are reported as "no filled form, exact copy". The mixed-modes warning is off in pair mode |
| 2 | Crossings | Checker: strokes that cross mid-segment count as a junction. SVG.md: crossing rule, and × spans less than + |
| 3 | Already-solid icons | CONSTRUCTION.md and the SKILL.md filled tree: "no filled form" now covers open lines, already-solid icons and slashed icons, and the filled sibling is an exact copy. The checker's warning names the exception |
| 4 | No gap location | Checker: gap and overlap messages give the coordinates |
| 6, 7 | Body parts, privacy | SKILL.md Step 1: a body branch (draw the tool or object, not the body part) and an "over the shoulder" privacy check |
| 8, 9 | Gestures, combinations | SKILL.md Step 1: gestures allowed when they are the target market's established control; combined shapes can form symbols |
| 10 | Context convention | SKILL.md Step 1: check which convention fits the product category |
| 11, 12 | Shared motifs, two objects | SKILL.md: rules for both (badge at least 5 across at 24, main object at 80–90%) |
| 13 | Organic forms | CONSTRUCTION.md "Forms": organic objects use continuous pinching curves |
| 14 | Stroke vs text | CONSTRUCTION.md: calibrate the stroke against the label beside it, then scale by ratio; the set overrides the table. SKILL.md Step 0 points to it |
| 15 | Cuts through the rim | CONSTRUCTION.md and the SVG.md Figma recipe: cuts always stay inside; restore the rim (reverses test #1's "through the edge") |
| 16 | Slash | CONSTRUCTION.md: cut ≈ 2.3× stroke, overrun 0.75, enlarge small inner details, filled = copy |
| 17, 18 | Dots | SVG.md: dots are filled circles (the stroke trick renders as a ring in Figma). CONSTRUCTION.md: standalone dots 1.5–2× stroke, punctuation dots 1.1–1.2× |
| 19 | Small sizes | SKILL.md size tree: start from the scaled master, then check it as a new icon; drop curls; redraw tangling neighbours |
| 20 | Fills that destroy | CONSTRUCTION.md: when the filled blind name changes, use a copy |
| + | Blind test | SKILL.md Step 5: three questions ("what is it?", "what else could it be in an app?", "could it read as a letter or number?") |
| + | Confusables | 14 new pairs from this test (ruler/battery, lock/bag, circle-!/info, "?"/2, bars/"00", eye-off/Ø, cards/wallet/copy, flag/danger, crescent+star, plane fold, head/fruit, filled lens/globe) |
| + | Process | Invocation: packs of about 5 with approval between them; mark "my pick" when working without approval. SVG.md: component sets with a `Style` property and copy variants |

## Not exercised yet
